Annotations

  1. 1Status refreshes automatically (at most 30 s old). 'Live' dot and last-updated time show freshness.
  2. 2Filter by type (segmented control) and by status (dropdown: Available, In use, Reserved, Out of order).
  3. 3Side panel: next booking, queue position and quiet times for this student.
  4. 4Each card shows its status as text plus a label (never colour alone). Book opens W6, Join queue opens W8, Report opens W10; clicking the machine ID opens details (W5).
  5. 5Out of order machines are hatched and cannot be booked.
  6. 6A finished cycle with laundry still inside shows Flag unattended (W11).
